### 🧑 Client

> I bought a split AC for Rs 38,000 from a brand's showroom in Ahmedabad in March 2023. The compressor failed in August 2023, inside the 1-year warranty (the compressor had a 5-year warranty). The company refused to repair it in October 2023, saying 'voltage damage'. Can I still file a consumer case now?

### ⚖️ Nyay Saathi — legal opinion

**Bottom line:** Your consumer complaint is time-barred as the 2-year limit from October 2023 has passed, unless you show sufficient cause.

**Case strength:** Weak case — The 2-year limitation period to file a consumer complaint has likely expired.

#### My reasoning

From what you have told me, your purchase makes you a consumer [1], and the refusal of warranty service is a deficiency in service. However, a consumer complaint must be filed within 2 years from the date the cause of action arose (October 2023 when they refused), which means the standard 2-year deadline has passed [2]. A late complaint is accepted only if the Commission is satisfied there was sufficient cause for the delay [2]. You can check with the National Consumer Helpline (1915) [3] or consult a district legal services authority [4] regarding any condonation of delay.
