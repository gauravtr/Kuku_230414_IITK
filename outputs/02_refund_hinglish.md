## Turn 1

### 🧑 Client

> Maine 18 October 2026 ko ek online marketplace (ShopKart) se Rs 15,999 ka phone liya. 20 October ko delivery hui aur 2 din mein screen pe green line aa gayi aur touch bhi theek se kaam nahi kar raha. Return request daali to 'physical damage' bol ke reject kar di, jabki phone pe ek scratch bhi nahi hai. Main bahut pareshan hu, ye phone maine apni pehli salary se liya tha. Ab kya karu?

### ⚖️ Nyay Saathi — legal opinion

**Bottom line:** Aapka case strong hai kyunki phone delivery ke 2 din mein kharab nikla aur company bina proof ke reject nahi kar sakti; National Consumer Helpline pe complaint karein.

**Case strength:** Strong case — Delivery ke turant baad defect aana aur bina scratch ke physical damage bolna company ki galat practice hai, lekin aapke paas clear photos/videos hone chahiye.

#### My reasoning

Aapne apni pehli salary se phone liya aur aisi pareshani aayi, ye samajhne layak hai. Law ke mutabiq agar online kharide gaye product mein delivery ke turant baad defect nikle, toh wo 'defect' mana jata hai [1]. ShopKart aur seller ko E-Commerce Rules ke tahat 48 ghante mein grievance acknowledge karni padti hai aur 1 mahine mein solve karna padta है [2]. Judgement [3] ke mutabiq deficiency prove karne ki zimmedari aap par hai, isliye aapke paas unboxing video, photos aur chat/ticket numbers hona zaroori hai. Aap District Consumer Commission mein bina kisi lawyer ke Rs 5 lakh tak ki value par bina kisi fee ke online e-Jagriti par case file kar sakte hain [4][5][6]. Supreme Court ke ruling [7] ke mutabiq aapko refund ke alawa mental harassment ke liye compensation bhi mil sakta hai.

#### Your action plan
