### ⚖️ Nyay Saathi — legal opinion

**Bottom line:** You have a strong claim for your Rs 50,000 deposit; do not argue on calls, communicate in writing instead.

**Case strength:** Strong case — Landlord cannot cut money for normal wear and tear or without actual proof of damage.

#### My reasoning

From what you've told me, your position is strong because routine repainting and deep cleaning fall under normal wear and tear, which a landlord cannot charge to a tenant unless real damage occurred [1]. Furthermore, landlords cannot deduct arbitrary amounts without proving actual loss [2], [3]. Since you get nervous on calls, stop calling him entirely. All communication must be in writing (WhatsApp or email) so you have a clear paper trail [4]. Ask him to provide an itemised list of actual damages and bills. We can prepare a formal legal notice if he refuses.

#### Your action plan
