### ⚖️ Nyay Saathi — legal opinion

#### My reasoning

From what you've told me, your position on the money is strong because the landlord has withheld your deposit without lawful justification after you vacated [1]. However, police will not register an FIR for cheating under the new criminal laws (which replaced the old IPC) because a delayed refund of a security deposit is a civil breach of contract, not a criminal offence. You can claim compensation for breach of contract [2]. Furthermore, under Supreme Court precedent in Maula Bux v. Union of India, a landlord cannot forfeit the entire deposit as a penalty unless there is actual provable loss or damage [3]. To protect your rights today, preserve the WhatsApp chat showing his refusal and gather your payment proof [4].

**Case strength:** Strong case — Landlord admitted refusal in writing, but this is a civil recovery matter, not a criminal case.

#### Your action plan

1. Send a formal written demand for the refund with a 7-15 day deadline — _this week_
2. Collect your bank statement showing the deposit payment and chat screenshots — _today_
