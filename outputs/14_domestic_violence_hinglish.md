#### Supreme Court rulings that fit your facts (You: Woman facing violence)

**In your favour**

- **Prabha Tyagi v. Kamlesh Devi** — Supreme Court · 2022 · (2022) 8 SCC 90; Crl. Appeal 511 of 2022
  - Why it fits your case Applies when she has left, or was made to leave, the shared home but still wants to claim residence or file. You said: …sasural se nikal diya. Abhi main apne mayke mein hu, Bhopal mein. Saas bhi gaali…
  - Held: A woman can enforce her right to reside in the shared household whether or not she actually lived there, and she can file under the DV Act even if she is no longer living with the respondents when she files, as long as the violence relates to the domestic relationship.
  - What it means for you: Aap mayke (Bhopal) se bhi DV Act ke tehat case file kar sakti hain chahe aap ab sasural mein na rehti ho.
  - Source: https://livelaw.in/top-stories/supreme-court-shared-house-hold-domestic-violence-prabha-tyagi-vs-kamlesh-devi-2022-livelaw-sc-474-198966
- **Satish Chander Ahuja v. Sneha Ahuja** — Supreme Court · 2020 · 2020 INSC 599; [2020] 12 SCR 189 (3 judges)
  - Why it fits your case Applies when she lived in the in-laws' house after marriage and is being kept out of it. You said: …unhone mujhe aur meri 4 saal ki beti ko sasural se nikal diya. Abhi main apne mayke mein…
  - Held: A 'shared household' is not limited to a house owned or rented by the husband. A wife can claim the right to live in the in-laws' house where she lived after marriage, even if her father-in-law owns it. This overruled the older narrow view in S.R. Batra.
  - What it means for you: Aapko sasural ke ghar mein rehne ka haq hai, chahe woh ghar kiska bhi naam par ho.
  - The right to residence is protected, but it is not an ownership right; the court balances it case by case.
  - Source: https://www.scobserver.in/journal/court-reinterprets-shared-household-under-the-domestic-violence-law/

**Against you: what the other side may cite**

- **Dara Lakshmi Narayana v. State of Telangana** — Supreme Court · 2024 · 2024 INSC 953; (2025) 3 SCC 735
  - Why it fits your case Applies when a police complaint or dowry-cruelty case may name the husband's relatives. You said: …unhone mujhe aur meri 4 saal ki beti ko sasural se nikal diya. Abhi main apne mayke mein…
  - Held: Quashed a dowry-cruelty FIR against the husband and his relatives because the allegations were vague and omnibus, with no dates or specific acts. The Court warned against misuse of the cruelty law to drag the whole family in.
  - It protects relatives against vague complaints; specific, dated allegations with proof are not affected.
  - Source: https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2024/12/11/supreme-court-quashes-false-dowry-case-misuse-section-498a-ipc/
- **Arnesh Kumar v. State of Bihar** — Supreme Court · 2014 · (2014) 8 SCC 273
  - Why it fits your case Applies when a dowry-cruelty police case, and possible arrest, is in the picture. You said: …Bhopal mein. Saas bhi gaali deti thi aur dahej ke liye taane deti thi. Mujhe samajh nahi…
  - Held: Police must not arrest automatically in dowry-cruelty cases (offences punishable up to 7 years). They must record why arrest is necessary, and magistrates must check this before ordering detention. The guidelines continue under the new criminal codes.
  - It limits arrest; it does not stop the case, the investigation or a DV Act application.
  - Source: https://legalfly.in/case-summary-arnesh-kumar-v-state-of-bihar/

#### Supreme Court rulings that fit your facts (You: Woman facing violence)

**In your favour**

- **Prabha Tyagi v. Kamlesh Devi** — Supreme Court · 2022 · (2022) 8 SCC 90; Crl. Appeal 511 of 2022
  - Why it fits your case Applies when she has left, or was made to leave, the shared home but still wants to claim residence or file. You said: …sasural se nikal diya. Abhi main apne mayke mein hu, Bhopal mein. Saas bhi gaali…
  - Held: A woman can enforce her right to reside in the shared household whether or not she actually lived there, and she can file under the DV Act even if she is no longer living with the respondents when she files, as long as the violence relates to the domestic relationship.
  - What it means for you: Aap mayke (Bhopal) se hi DV case file kar sakti hain, sasural mein rehna zaruri nahi hai.
  - Source: https://livelaw.in/top-stories/supreme-court-shared-house-hold-domestic-violence-prabha-tyagi-vs-kamlesh-devi-2022-livelaw-sc-474-198966
- **Rajnesh v. Neha** — Supreme Court · 2020 · (2021) 2 SCC 324
  - Why it fits your case Applies when money for her own or the children's upkeep (maintenance) is in issue. You said: …sasur ke naam pe hai? Beti ke school aur kharche ka kya? Mere gehne kaise wapas milenge?…
  - Held: Laid down binding guidelines for maintenance: both sides must file an affidavit disclosing income, assets and liabilities; maintenance runs from the date the application is filed; courts must decide interim maintenance quickly. The Supreme Court has since ordered this judgment re-circulated to all judges.
  - What it means for you: Pati ki 70k income ke aadhar par aapko aur beti ke liye achha maintenance mil sakta hai.
  - The disclosure affidavit applies to her too: she must also disclose her own income and assets.
  - Source: https://www.mondaq.com/supreme-court-clarifies-the-law-on-maintenance/1034570
- **Satish Chander Ahuja v. Sneha Ahuja** — Supreme Court · 2020 · 2020 INSC 599; [2020] 12 SCR 189 (3 judges)
  - Why it fits your case Applies when she lived in the in-laws' house after marriage and is being kept out of it. You said: …unhone mujhe aur meri 4 saal ki beti ko sasural se nikal diya. Abhi main apne mayke mein…
  - Held: A 'shared household' is not limited to a house owned or rented by the husband. A wife can claim the right to live in the in-laws' house where she lived after marriage, even if her father-in-law owns it. This overruled the older narrow view in S.R. Batra.
  - What it means for you: Sasural ka ghar agar sasur ke naam bhi ho, tab bhi aapko wahan rehne ka haq mil sakta hai.
  - The right to residence is protected, but it is not an ownership right; the court balances it case by case.
  - Source: https://www.scobserver.in/journal/court-reinterprets-shared-household-under-the-domestic-violence-law/

**Against you: what the other side may cite**

- **Dara Lakshmi Narayana v. State of Telangana** — Supreme Court · 2024 · 2024 INSC 953; (2025) 3 SCC 735
  - Why it fits your case Applies when a police complaint or dowry-cruelty case may name the husband's relatives. You said: …unhone mujhe aur meri 4 saal ki beti ko sasural se nikal diya. Abhi main apne mayke mein…
  - Held: Quashed a dowry-cruelty FIR against the husband and his relatives because the allegations were vague and omnibus, with no dates or specific acts. The Court warned against misuse of the cruelty law to drag the whole family in.
  - It protects relatives against vague complaints; specific, dated allegations with proof are not affected.
  - Source: https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2024/12/11/supreme-court-quashes-false-dowry-case-misuse-section-498a-ipc/
- **Arnesh Kumar v. State of Bihar** — Supreme Court · 2014 · (2014) 8 SCC 273
  - Why it fits your case Applies when a dowry-cruelty police case, and possible arrest, is in the picture. You said: …Bhopal mein. Saas bhi gaali deti thi aur dahej ke liye taane deti thi. Mujhe samajh nahi…
  - Held: Police must not arrest automatically in dowry-cruelty cases (offences punishable up to 7 years). They must record why arrest is necessary, and magistrates must check this before ordering detention. The guidelines continue under the new criminal codes.
  - It limits arrest; it does not stop the case, the investigation or a DV Act application.
  - Source: https://legalfly.in/case-summary-arnesh-kumar-v-state-of-bihar/
